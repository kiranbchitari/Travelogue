Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Travelogue is an offline-first travel diary and expense manager for Android. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it. The short version: your data stays on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives it.

Data you enter

Trips, itineraries, expenses, diary entries, photos, documents, contacts and settings you add are stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. Travelogue has no account system, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting, and it does not sell or share your data with anyone.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you tap Back up to Google Drive or Restore from Google Drive, the app asks you to sign in with Google and grant the drive.file permission. With it, Travelogue can only see and manage files it created itself. It cannot read any other file in your Drive.

  • Backups are ZIP files saved in App Backups › Travelogue Backups in your own Google Drive and count against your own Drive storage.
  • Your phone uploads and downloads these files directly with Google. Nothing passes through, or is stored on, any server run by the developer.
  • The app uses the access token Google issues only to read and write those backup files, and does not keep your Google password.

Travelogue's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google's handling of data in your Drive is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Exports you create

When you export a trip or a full backup as a ZIP, it is saved wherever you choose. What happens to that file afterwards (for example, sharing it) is up to you.

Android device backup

Depending on your phone's settings, Android's own backup service may include app data in your device backup with Google. You can manage this in your phone's system settings.

Your control

  • Delete any item in the app at any time, or use Settings › Clear all app data. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the device.
  • Delete Drive backups by removing the Travelogue Backups folder or its files in Google Drive.
  • Revoke Travelogue's Drive access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Children

Travelogue is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
