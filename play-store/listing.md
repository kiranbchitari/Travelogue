# Travelogue – Play Store listing

## App name (30)
Travelogue

## Short description (80)
Offline travel diary, day-by-day itinerary planner and trip expense tracker.

## Full description (4000)
Travelogue keeps every journey in one beautiful, private place: the plan, the people, the money and the memories.

PLAN EVERY DAY
• Build a day-by-day itinerary with times, places and notes
• Drag to reorder activities and tick them off as you go
• Keep hotels, bookings, places and useful contacts with each trip

TRACK SPENDING, IN ANY CURRENCY
• Log expenses by category, day and who paid
• Spend in yen, euros or dollars and see totals in your home currency
• Set a budget, split costs with travel companions and see who owes whom
• Financial summary with cost per person, daily average and category breakdown

KEEP THE MEMORIES
• Write a travel diary with tags
• Add photos and documents to each trip
• Browse a scrapbook of moments, favourites and stories across all your trips

PLAN TIME OFF
• Holiday planner with Indian public holidays
• Mark your days off and your partner's to find long weekends you can travel together

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works offline. Everything is stored on your phone
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Optional backup to your own Google Drive, into an "App Backups › Travelogue Backups" folder. Travelogue can only see the files it creates there
• Export any trip, or everything, as a ZIP and restore it on another phone
